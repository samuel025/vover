How it works

From raw footage to narrated video

Four steps, one page. No timeline to learn and no separate recorder to line up.

Upload your video

Drop in an MP4, MOV or WebM. The original sound is muted so it never leaks into your mic.

Paste your script

Drop in your text. The teleprompter spreads it across the video and scrolls with it.

Record your voice

Press record, get a three second countdown, then read the highlighted line while the video plays.

Export the MP4

Your voice replaces the original audio. Download a standard MP4 that plays everywhere.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the studio and start. Your work is saved in your own browser so a refresh does not lose it.

What happens to the original sound?

It is muted while you record and removed on export. If you prefer, you can keep it as a quiet background track.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

For most MP4 and MOV files, no. The export is built in your browser, so the video never leaves your device and there is no size limit beyond what your computer can handle. Some formats, such as WebM, and the "keep original audio" option are processed on our server instead.

Which video formats work?

MP4, MOV and WebM. MP4 and MOV keep their original picture quality. Other formats are converted to H.264.

Can I fix a mistake without re-recording everything?

Yes. Pause, play back to find the spot, trim your voice there and continue recording from that point.

Do I need a special microphone?

No. Any built-in or headset microphone works. Pick it in the options panel and watch the level meter before you record.
